## 5. Voice System

### Voice

- Cinematic, precise, restrained.
- Human before lore.
- Suggest mystery without pretending to explain it.
- Use short declarative lines and controlled questions.

### Never use

- Empty hype: “the greatest,” “mind-blowing,” “epic content.”
- Generic horror vocabulary unless the story genuinely requires it.
- Lore exposition that reveals the Archive's mechanism.
- Public names or final meanings for the eighteen glyphs.
- Arabic copy on the global NOVAFRAX / ARCHIVE OF SHADOWS profiles.
- Unlocked dates, promises, or production claims.
